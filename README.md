# Check electricity and gas prices (Victoria)

Find the cheapest electricity and gas plans for your home, using your own meter data and gas bills.
Use it here: [yobin-tim.github.io/vic-energy-plan-check](https://yobin-tim.github.io/vic-energy-plan-check/)

1. Enter your postcode.
2. Add the usage file from your electricity network's website, or try a sample home.
3. See every plan ranked by what you would pay, cheapest first.
4. Compare up to four plans side by side, month by month.

For gas, type the dates and the gas used (in MJ) from one or two bills, or try a sample
home. The Gas page also compares electricity and gas from one retailer with the cheapest of
each.

Your usage file and your gas bills never leave your device. Plan prices come from the government's
public plan list.

I made this for my own household and shared it in case it helps someone else. The costs
are estimates, not financial advice. It covers all five Victorian electricity networks and all
three gas networks; so far, only United Energy's usage file has been tried with it.
